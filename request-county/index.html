Request your county

We add counties one at a time, and requests decide the order. If your county is not covered yet, tell us where you work and we will email you if we open it.

We only open counties in our 10 focus states for now: California, Texas, Florida, New York, Pennsylvania, Illinois, Ohio, Georgia, North Carolina, and Michigan. You can still ask for a county in another state — we count every request, and the count is what makes the case for widening the map.

Asking is free, and everything on this site stays free to read. This list is only for county requests — we will not send you anything else.
