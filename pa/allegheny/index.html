Source: City of Pittsburgh open data (Western Pennsylvania Regional Data Center) — PLI permits issued by the City of Pittsburgh. Source last updated: November 1, 2026 · Our last pull: November 1, 2026 · Figures are as reported by the City of Pittsburgh.

Coverage: this report covers building permits issued by the City of Pittsburgh. Allegheny County's other municipalities issue their own permits through their own systems and are not in this feed, so totals here are Pittsburgh totals — labeled as such, never blended. Two facts about the city's data shape the figures. The register also carries plan-review meeting records, which are not permits and are not counted here. And the register's project-value field repeats one project's declared value across its separate permits — the largest figure in it sits on an occupant-load placard, not a construction permit — so this report publishes permit counts, not a dollar total that would misstate the work.
