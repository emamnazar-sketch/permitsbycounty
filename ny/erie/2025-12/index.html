Buffalo Building Permits — December 2025

Every building permit issued by the City of Buffalo in December 2025: counts, reported value, permits by trade, and the month's biggest projects.

Source: City of Buffalo open data — permits issued by the Department of Permit & Inspection Services. Source last updated: November 12, 2026 · Our last pull: November 12, 2026 · Figures are as reported by the City of Buffalo.

Coverage: this report covers building permits issued by the City of Buffalo (Department of Permit & Inspection Services). Erie County's other cities and towns issue their own permits through their own systems and are not in this feed, so totals here are Buffalo totals — labeled as such, never blended. Two facts about the city's data shape the figures. The dataset is an issued-permits register — every permit in it carries an issue date and the city's own notes state it holds no pending permits — and it carries one row for every permit, so each permit is counted once, by its permit number. And the value the city states for a permit is the value of the work it covers: for a phased project the city states the whole project cost on the first permit secured, and the trade permits that follow carry their own scope values — the largest projects are shown with their descriptions, so the shape of each figure stays visible.
