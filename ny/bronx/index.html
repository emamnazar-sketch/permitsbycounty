Building Permits in Bronx County (the Bronx), New York

Every building permit issued by the New York City Department of Buildings in the Bronx — counted, valued, and sorted by trade. Free, and updated daily.

Source: New York City open data — DOB NOW: Build, approved permits (NYC Department of Buildings). Source last updated: November 26, 2026 · Our last pull: November 27, 2026 · Figures are as reported by the NYC Department of Buildings.

Coverage: this report covers building permits issued by the New York City Department of Buildings (DOB NOW) in the Bronx (Bronx County). The city's other boroughs publish as their own county reports: New York County (Manhattan), Kings County (Brooklyn), Queens County, and Richmond County (Staten Island). Two counting rules matter here. DOB's feed lists one row for every work type an issuance covers, so we count each issuance once. And the estimated cost DOB publishes belongs to the whole job filing and repeats on each of its issuances, so we count each filing's cost once, on its most recent issuance. DOB NOW excludes electrical, elevator, and limited-alteration permits — DOB publishes those in separate datasets — so no electrical figures appear in this report.

Questions, answered from the data

How many building permits were issued in the Bronx in October 2026?

1,510 building permits were issued in the Bronx in October 2026, with a reported project value of $245,114,659, as reported by the NYC Department of Buildings.

How many building permits were issued in the Bronx in the last 12 months?

17,747 building permits were issued in the Bronx in the 12 months ending November 2026 (November 25, 2026), with a combined reported project value of $4,161,699,435, as reported by the NYC Department of Buildings.

What is the most common type of building permit in the Bronx?

Scaffolding permits are the most common classified type: 4,309 issued in the last 12 months (24.3% of all permits), as reported by the NYC Department of Buildings.
