Source: New York City open data — DOB NOW: Build, approved permits (NYC Department of Buildings). Source last updated: November 27, 2026 · Our last pull: November 28, 2026 · Figures are as reported by the NYC Department of Buildings.

Coverage: this report covers building permits issued by the New York City Department of Buildings (DOB NOW) in Queens (Queens County). The city's other boroughs publish as their own county reports: New York County (Manhattan), Kings County (Brooklyn), Bronx County, and Richmond County (Staten Island). Two counting rules matter here. DOB's feed lists one row for every work type an issuance covers, so we count each issuance once. And the estimated cost DOB publishes belongs to the whole job filing and repeats on each of its issuances, so we count each filing's cost once, on its most recent issuance. DOB NOW excludes electrical, elevator, and limited-alteration permits — DOB publishes those in separate datasets — so no electrical figures appear in this report.

Questions, answered from the data

How many building permits were issued in Queens in October 2026?

2,444 building permits were issued in Queens in October 2026, with a reported project value of $1,094,225,195, as reported by the NYC Department of Buildings.

How many building permits were issued in Queens in the last 12 months?

30,111 building permits were issued in Queens in the 12 months ending November 2026 (November 25, 2026), with a combined reported project value of $6,742,273,954, as reported by the NYC Department of Buildings.

What is the most common type of building permit in Queens?

Plumbing permits are the most common classified type: 4,724 issued in the last 12 months (15.7% of all permits), as reported by the NYC Department of Buildings.
