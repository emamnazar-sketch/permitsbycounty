Decatur Building Permits — November 2026

Every building permit issued by the DeKalb County in November 2026: counts, permits by trade, and the month's most recent permits.

Source: DeKalb County GIS — building permit applications (Hansen register). Source last updated: November 7, 2026 · Our last pull: November 7, 2026 · Figures are as reported by the DeKalb County.

Coverage: this report covers building permits recorded in DeKalb County's permitting system — the county issues through it for unincorporated DeKalb County, and the register also carries the county's permit reviews for several incorporated cities it serves. DeKalb cities that run their own permitting systems are not in this feed, so totals here are DeKalb County system totals — labeled as such, never blended. Three facts about the county's data shape the figures. The register lists some permits more than once — exact mirror copies of the same application — so each application is counted once, by its application number. Its "Owner Change Only" records are changes of record that authorize no work, and they are not counted as permits. And the register's declared project-value field repeats one project's figure across its separate permits — the same large figure stands on a new-construction permit and on a plumbing permit for one address — so this report publishes permit counts, not a dollar total that would misstate the work.
