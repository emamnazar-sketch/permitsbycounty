Coverage: this report covers permits issued by the City of Detroit (Buildings, Safety Engineering and Environmental Department — BSEED). Wayne County's other municipalities issue their own permits through their own systems and are not in this feed, so totals here are Detroit totals — labeled as such, never blended. Three facts about the city's data shape the figures. Detroit publishes two registers — building permits and trade permits — and this report combines both, counting each permit once by its permit number. BSEED demolition permits are published in a separate city dataset and are not in this report. And the city states an estimated cost of work on its building permits but publishes no cost field on its trade permits, so the reported value below is the building permits' stated total.
