Every building permit issued by the City of Columbus in September 2026: counts, permits by trade, and the month's most recent permits.

Source: City of Columbus open data — building permits issued by the Department of Building and Zoning Services. Source last updated: November 14, 2026 · Our last pull: November 14, 2026 · Figures are as reported by the City of Columbus.

Coverage: this report covers building permits issued by the City of Columbus (Department of Building and Zoning Services). Columbus also extends into Delaware and Fairfield counties, and other Franklin County jurisdictions publish their permits separately and are not in this feed, so totals here are Columbus totals — labeled as such, never blended. Two facts about the city's data shape the figures. The layer is an issued-permits register — every permit in it carries an issue date — and it carries one row for every permit, so each permit is counted once, by its permit number. And the city's stated project-value field does not hold up as a per-permit value: one drainage permit states more than six billion dollars, project values repeat across a project's separate permits, and the trade permits state almost no value at all — so this report publishes permit counts, not a dollar total that would misstate the work.
