Source: City of Cincinnati open data — building permits from the Buildings & Inspections Department. Source last updated: October 18, 2026 · Our last pull: October 18, 2026 · Figures are as reported by the City of Cincinnati.

Coverage: this report covers building permits issued by the City of Cincinnati (Buildings & Inspections Department). Other Hamilton County jurisdictions publish their permits separately and are not in this feed, so totals here are Cincinnati totals — labeled as such, never blended. Two facts about the city's data shape the counts. The dataset lists one row for every work component a permit covers, so we count each permit once and add its components' stated costs together for its value. And the dataset carries no electrical permits at all, so no electrical figures appear in this report.

Questions, answered from the data

How many building permits were issued in Cincinnati in September 2026?

763 building permits were issued in Cincinnati in September 2026, with a reported project value of $115,264,790, as reported by the City of Cincinnati.

How many building permits were issued in Cincinnati in the last 12 months?

9,788 building permits were issued in Cincinnati in the 12 months ending October 2026 (October 16, 2026), with a combined reported project value of $1,240,768,379, as reported by the City of Cincinnati.

What is the most common type of building permit in Cincinnati?

Plumbing permits are the most common classified type: 3,094 issued in the last 12 months (31.6% of all permits), as reported by the City of Cincinnati.
