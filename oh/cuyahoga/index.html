Every building permit issued by the City of Cleveland — counted, valued, and sorted by trade. Free, and updated weekly.

Source: City of Cleveland open data — building permits issued by the Department of Building and Housing. Source last updated: November 8, 2026 · Our last pull: November 14, 2026 · Figures are as reported by the City of Cleveland.

Coverage: this report covers building permits issued by the City of Cleveland (Department of Building and Housing). Cuyahoga County's other cities issue their own permits through their own systems and are not in this feed, so totals here are Cleveland totals — labeled as such, never blended. Two facts about the city's data shape the figures. The dataset is an issued-permits register — every permit in it carries an issue date — and it carries one row for every permit, so each permit is counted once, by its permit number. And the city reloads this dataset in a weekly batch on Sundays, so the freshest permits can run about a week behind the calendar — the stamps above show the source's own dates. The register's board-up permits — issued permits to secure vacant properties — are counted as the permits they are; they state no job value ($0 as reported).

Questions, answered from the data

How many building permits were issued in Cleveland in October 2026?

1,333 building permits were issued in Cleveland in October 2026, with a reported project value of $282,555,666, as reported by the City of Cleveland.

How many building permits were issued in Cleveland in the last 12 months?

14,455 building permits were issued in Cleveland in the 12 months ending November 2026 (November 7, 2026), with a combined reported project value of $1,719,595,874, as reported by the City of Cleveland.

What is the most common type of building permit in Cleveland?

Electrical permits are the most common classified type: 2,684 issued in the last 12 months (18.6% of all permits), as reported by the City of Cleveland.
