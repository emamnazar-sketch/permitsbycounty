Coverage: this report covers building permits issued by the City of Boston (Inspectional Services Department). Suffolk County also contains Chelsea, Revere, and Winthrop, which publish their permits separately and are not in this feed, so totals here are Boston totals — labeled as such, never blended. Values are the declared project values applicants reported to the city, printed as reported; a Certificate of Occupancy restates its project's declared value, so one project's value can appear on both its building permit and its certificate.

Questions, answered from the data

How many building permits were issued in Boston in September 2026?

3,145 building permits were issued in Boston in September 2026, with a reported project value of $1,462,293,769, as reported by the City of Boston.

How many building permits were issued in Boston in the last 12 months?

36,674 building permits were issued in Boston in the 12 months ending October 2026 (October 20, 2026), with a combined reported project value of $12,785,493,971, as reported by the City of Boston.

What is the most common type of building permit in Boston?

Electrical permits are the most common classified type: 10,813 issued in the last 12 months (29.5% of all permits), as reported by the City of Boston.
