Coverage: this report covers building permits issued by the City and County of San Francisco (a consolidated city-county, so the feed covers the whole county). One counting rule matters here: San Francisco's Department of Building Inspection publishes one row for every address or lot a permit touches, so one permit can appear several times in the raw feed. We count each permit once, at the address the department flags as primary — counts on this page are permits, never raw feed rows.

Questions, answered from the data

How many building permits were issued in San Francisco in September 2026?

1,298 building permits were issued in San Francisco in September 2026, with a reported project value of $275,984,819, as reported by the City and County of San Francisco.

How many building permits were issued in San Francisco in the last 12 months?

20,170 building permits were issued in San Francisco in the 12 months ending October 2026 (October 16, 2026), with a combined reported project value of $3,536,538,582, as reported by the City and County of San Francisco.

What is the most common type of building permit in San Francisco?

Roofing permits are the most common classified type: 2,676 issued in the last 12 months (13.3% of all permits), as reported by the City and County of San Francisco.
