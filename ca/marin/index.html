Building Permits in Marin County, California

Every building permit processed by Marin County — counted, valued, and sorted by trade. Free, and updated daily.

Source: Marin County open data — building permits processed by the Marin County Community Development Agency. Source last updated: November 20, 2026 · Our last pull: November 20, 2026 · Figures are as reported by the County of Marin.

Coverage: this report covers building permits processed by Marin County (Community Development Agency) — the county's own permitting jurisdiction, which includes unincorporated Marin and the county-served areas inside its cities and towns, from Mill Valley and San Rafael to Point Reyes Station. Marin's incorporated cities also run their own permit counters for work outside the county's jurisdiction; those permits are not in this feed, so totals here are Marin County totals — labeled as such, never blended. The dataset carries one row for every permit, so each permit is counted once, by its permit number. The value the county states for a permit is its stated construction value for the work the permit covers, published here as reported.

Questions, answered from the data

How many building permits were issued in San Rafael in October 2026?

391 building permits were issued in San Rafael in October 2026, with a reported project value of $40,912,928, as reported by the County of Marin.

How many building permits were issued in San Rafael in the last 12 months?

4,271 building permits were issued in San Rafael in the 12 months ending November 2026 (November 19, 2026), with a combined reported project value of $298,736,795, as reported by the County of Marin.

What is the most common type of building permit in San Rafael?

Electrical permits are the most common classified type: 981 issued in the last 12 months (23.0% of all permits), as reported by the County of Marin.
