Source: City of Los Angeles open data — building permits issued by the Department of Building and Safety (LADBS). Source last updated: November 9, 2026 · Our last pull: November 14, 2026 · Figures are as reported by the City of Los Angeles.

Coverage: this report covers building permits issued by the City of Los Angeles (Department of Building and Safety). Los Angeles County's other cities issue their own permits through their own systems and are not in this feed, so totals here are City of Los Angeles totals — labeled as such, never blended. Two facts about the city's data shape the figures. This dataset is the department's building permits: LADBS publishes its electrical and mechanical permits in separate datasets, so licensed-trade figures here are read only from each building permit's own type and scope text, and only where the signal is unambiguous (re-roof permits are the clear case). And the city reloads this dataset in a weekly batch, so the freshest permits can run about a week behind the calendar — the stamps above show the source's own dates.

Questions, answered from the data

How many building permits were issued in Los Angeles in October 2026?

6,161 building permits were issued in Los Angeles in October 2026, with a reported project value of $469,627,078, as reported by the City of Los Angeles.

How many building permits were issued in Los Angeles in the last 12 months?

65,151 building permits were issued in Los Angeles in the 12 months ending November 2026 (November 7, 2026), with a combined reported project value of $9,550,262,411, as reported by the City of Los Angeles.

What is the most common type of building permit in Los Angeles?

Roofing permits are the most common classified type: 14,441 issued in the last 12 months (22.2% of all permits), as reported by the City of Los Angeles.
