Coverage: this report covers building permits issued by the City of Asheville. Buncombe County's own permits for the unincorporated county have no published feed, and the county's other towns publish separately, so totals here are City of Asheville totals — labeled as such, never blended. Three facts about the city's data shape the figures. The city publishes no true issue date, so this report counts only permits standing in a status that proves issuance (issued, certificate issued, finaled, reissued, closed after issuance, expired, or temporary certificate issued), by the month each permit was opened — applications still in review, awaiting the applicant or payment, revoked, or denied are not counted, and recent months grow as opened applications reach an issued status. Record types that authorize no building work — short-term home stays, sound exceedance, and temporary publishing permits (34 opened in the last 12 months) — are not counted either. And the city's stated job value is blank or zero on effectively every record (a value above zero appears on 3 of 66,667 records), so this report publishes permit counts, trades, and monthly volumes only. The register's record-name field often names a person and is never pulled; a business name is shown only where the city's published business name carries a business signal, and individual-looking names are dropped, never printed as a contractor.
