Wilmington Building Permits — October 2026

Every building permit issued by the New Hanover County in October 2026: counts, permits by trade, and the month's most recent permits.

Source: New Hanover County open data — building permits. Source last updated: November 25, 2026 · Our last pull: November 28, 2026 · Figures are as reported by the New Hanover County.

Coverage: this report covers building permits issued by New Hanover County — the county's own permit register, which covers Wilmington, Carolina Beach, Wrightsville Beach, Kure Beach, Castle Hayne, and the unincorporated county, so totals here are the county register's totals — labeled as such, never blended. Three facts about the county's data shape the figures. The county states one project value for a whole project and repeats that same figure on every separate permit the project pulls — one Wilmington hospital project's stated value sits, unchanged, on both its mechanical and its electrical permit — so a dollar total built from it would count the same project again and again; this report publishes permit counts, trades, and monthly volumes only. The register also carries record types that authorize no construction — county health-department operating licenses and inspections (food, lodging, pools, body art), fire operational permits, zoning permits, a facility license, and an occupancy certification (994 permits in the last 12 months) — those are left out of this report and named here, so the counts cover permits for physical work only. And two legacy permits in the register carry plainly wrong future issue dates (2027); they fall outside the window and are not counted.
