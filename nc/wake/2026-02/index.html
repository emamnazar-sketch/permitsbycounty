Coverage: this report covers building permits issued by the City of Raleigh. Other Wake County towns publish their permits separately and are not in this feed, so totals here are Raleigh totals — labeled as such, never blended. Two facts about the city's data shape the figures. The dataset is the city's building permits — Raleigh publishes its plumbing, electrical, and mechanical permits in separate datasets — so the trade figures here are read from each building permit's own work class, use, and scope description. And project values are the applicants' estimated project costs, printed as reported: identical buildings in one development can state the same estimated cost on each of their permits.
