Questions, answered from the data

How many building permits were issued in Chicago in October 2026?

2,759 building permits were issued in Chicago in October 2026, with a reported project value of $981,247,189, as reported by the City of Chicago.

How many building permits were issued in Chicago in the last 12 months?

33,931 building permits were issued in Chicago in the 12 months ending November 2026 (November 11, 2026), with a combined reported project value of $11,741,546,019, as reported by the City of Chicago.

What is the most common type of building permit in Chicago?

Electrical permits are the most common classified type: 8,153 issued in the last 12 months (24.0% of all permits), as reported by the City of Chicago.
