Source: City of Naperville open data — building permits issued by the City of Naperville. Source last updated: November 10, 2026 · Our last pull: November 24, 2026 · Figures are as reported by the City of Naperville.

Coverage: this report covers building permits issued by the City of Naperville. Naperville straddles DuPage and Will counties, and the city publishes its permit register as one — so totals here are City of Naperville totals, labeled as such, never blended. Other DuPage County jurisdictions (Wheaton, Downers Grove, Elmhurst, and the county's own unincorporated permitting) publish their permits separately and are not in this feed. Three facts about the city's data shape the figures. The city's register mixes every city department, so this report counts only the building scope — the residential, commercial, sub-permit, sign, and elevator families — and only permits standing in a status that proves issuance (issued, complete, expired, temporary occupancy, or final inspection passed); the finance, public-works, fire, truck-route, right-of-way, and other department families, and applications still in review, awaiting fees, void, or cancelled, are not counted. The feed publishes no contractor name, so none is shown. And the city's data reaches us in batches rather than day by day — the stamps above show the source's own dates, and the newest permits in a batch can run some weeks behind the calendar. Project values are the applicants' stated valuations, printed as reported.
