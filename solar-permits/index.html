What counts as a solar permit here

A permit lands on this page when its type or description names solar work — rooftop panels, a ground-mount array, a panel upgrade. Battery work filed inside a solar permit rides along with it; a battery filed on its own electrical permit shows up on the electrical page instead. A new build that will carry panels later is not counted here until the solar permit itself is filed.

Read a solar permit as a map, not a lead

A solar permit usually means the job is already sold. The record often names the installer who won it, so the permit itself is rarely an open lead. What it gives you is the map: which neighborhoods are buying, how fast installs are moving county by county, and which installers are busiest.

One date explains the shape of the last year: the federal residential solar tax credit ended on December 31, 2025, and the two busiest months in this record are the two right before it — 1,268 permits in November 2025 and 1,228 in December 2025. Installs did not stop; they settled to a lower monthly pace.

The permit that sells solar is the new roof. A homeowner with a fresh roof has a roof that can carry panels for decades, and the decision window runs about 30 to 60 days after the roof work — so the roofing report is this page's closest companion.

Questions, answered from the data

How many solar permits were issued in the last 12 months?

11,392 solar permits were issued across the 23 counties PermitsByCounty covers in the last 12 months. Queens County issued the most, with 2,754. Each county report carries its own coverage dates and source.

Are solar permits themselves good leads?

Usually not as direct leads. A solar permit normally means an installer already sold that job, and the record often names that installer. Grouped by county and installer, solar permits still show you where solar is moving and who your competitors are.

What is the best permit signal for solar?

A new roof permit. The homeowner has a fresh roof that can carry panels for years, and the solar decision window runs about 30 to 60 days after the roof work. New builds and panel upgrades come next.

Does a permit give me the homeowner's phone number?

Usually no. A permit normally carries the address, the work, the dates, and the contractor of record — not a verified homeowner phone number. If a seller promises one on every row, ask which record it came from.
