What counts as an electrical permit here

A permit lands on this page when it is an electrical permit or its scope names electrical work — a service upgrade, a panel upgrade, an EV charger. Charger permits count as electrical because that is what they are: a new circuit, and often a panel check before it.

Two big sources do not feed this page. New York City files its electrical permits in a separate dataset from its building permits, and Los Angeles publishes its electrical permits separately too — so New York and Los Angeles appear in the other trade reports but not in this one. The gap is in the source data, not in the work: plenty of electrical work happens in both cities; their building-permit feeds just do not carry it.

Upgrades are the early signal

Straight electrical permits mean work now. Panel and service upgrades mean work soon: a bigger panel is usually the first step before something else — a remodel, a charger, a hot tub, a solar install — so an upgrade permit is one of the earliest public signs that a household is about to spend on its home.

The trade barely sleeps. Monthly electrical permits across the covered counties ran between 2,932 and 8,998 over the last year — no real off-season, just a steady hum underneath every other trade's work.

Questions, answered from the data

How many electrical permits were issued in the last 12 months?

101,681 electrical permits were issued across the 22 counties PermitsByCounty covers in the last 12 months. Travis County issued the most, with 17,317. Each county report carries its own coverage dates and source.

Why is New York City missing from the electrical table?

New York City files electrical permits in a separate dataset from its building permits, and Los Angeles publishes electrical permits separately too. Those feeds are not in this report yet, so the table covers the counties whose building-permit feeds carry electrical work.

What does a panel upgrade permit tell me?

A service or panel upgrade usually means the home's power needs just grew — a remodel, an EV charger, a hot tub, or a solar install can all sit behind one. It is one of the earliest public signs that a household is spending on its electrical system.

Are EV charger permits counted as electrical?

Yes. A charger permit is electrical work — a new circuit, often a panel check first — so charger permits are counted with electrical permits here.
