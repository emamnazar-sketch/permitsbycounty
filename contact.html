Corrections to a published figure

If a number on a report does not match the official record, we want to know. Include the report's address (URL), the figure in question, and a link to the official record if you have one. Our Corrections Policy explains what happens next — in short: we check, we fix or remove, and we note the change.

Questions about sources and methodology

Wondering where a figure came from, or how a county's feed works? Most answers live on our Data Sources & Methodology page. Anything it does not cover, ask us directly.

Press, data, and partnerships

Journalists working with our county figures, jurisdictions asking about their own feeds, and businesses interested in the reserved services (bulk data, API access, alerts) are all welcome to write.
