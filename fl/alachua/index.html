Building Permits in Alachua County, Florida

Every building permit issued by Alachua County — counted and sorted by trade and month. Free, and updated nightly.

Source: Alachua County open data — building permits issued by Alachua County Growth Management. Source last updated: November 17, 2026 · Our last pull: November 18, 2026 · Figures are as reported by the Alachua County.

Coverage: this report covers building permits issued by Alachua County — the permits the county itself issues, for unincorporated Alachua County and county-served areas. The City of Gainesville and the county's other cities issue their own permits through their own systems and they are not in this feed, so totals here are county-issued totals — labeled as such, never blended. Three facts about the county's data shape the figures. The county's layer carries no project-cost field at all, so this report publishes permit counts, trades, and monthly volumes only — there is no dollar figure to report. The layer also publishes no city or ZIP for a permit, so addresses appear exactly as the county prints them, with nothing added. And the register includes a few record types that authorize no construction — zoning-verification letters, pre-application screenings, development-review and land-use applications, public-works administrative reviews, and temporary-use permits (92 permits in the last 12 months) — those are left out of this report and named here, so the counts cover permits for physical work only.
