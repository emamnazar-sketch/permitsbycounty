Source: Miami-Dade County open data — building permits issued by Miami-Dade County. Source last updated: October 27, 2026 · Our last pull: October 28, 2026 · Figures are as reported by the Miami-Dade County.

Coverage: this report covers building permits issued by Miami-Dade County — the permits the county itself issues, for unincorporated Miami-Dade County and county projects. The county's incorporated cities (Miami, Hialeah, and the other municipalities) issue their own permits and are not in this feed, so totals here are county-issued totals — labeled as such, never blended. Two facts about the county's data shape the figures. Trade permits issued under a master building permit are separate county permits with their own permit numbers and are counted as such. And the county's estimated-value field repeats a master permit's value on its sub-permits and carries corrupted figures at the top end (24 permits state more than two billion dollars), so this report publishes permit counts, not a dollar total that would misstate the work.
