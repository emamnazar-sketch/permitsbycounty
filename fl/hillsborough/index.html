Coverage: this report covers building permits issued by Hillsborough County — the permits the county itself issues, for unincorporated Hillsborough County and county-served areas. The City of Tampa issues its own permits through its own system and they are not in this feed, so totals here are county-issued totals — labeled as such, never blended. Three facts about the county's data shape the figures. The county's published layer merges each permit's issued record with its certificate-of-occupancy record, so this report counts only the issued records — each permit once, by its permit number. The county names no licensed trades in its permit families, so trade figures come from each permit's own scope text, read only for strong signals (a re-roof, a sign installation, an HVAC system); pools, screen enclosures, sheds, additions, and renovations stay in General & Other. And the county's stated project-value field repeats one project's figure across its separate permits and states ordinary single-family homes at 95 million dollars and more, so this report publishes permit counts, not a dollar total that would misstate the work.
